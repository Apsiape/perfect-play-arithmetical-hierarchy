# Version 1.2.0 release notes

6 November 2026.

- Theorem A is now proved in Lean with no hypotheses. The proof uses only Lean's three standard axioms, passes the kernel checkers leanchecker and nanoda, and is checked by the comparator challenge, which imports only Mathlib. The finite stages of the $\Sigma^0_2$ upper bounds are shown to be uniformly $\Pi^0_1$ by exact rational grid certificates: every satisfiable stage or finite entropy relaxation has a solution with all coordinates in $[-1,1]$; each constraint is Lipschitz in the maximum norm on that box; and compactness of the box makes the certificate complete. Version 1.1.0 instead took a special case of Tarski's 1951 decision theorem for real arithmetic as an explicit hypothesis. That hypothesis is removed and the Tarski reference is dropped. Appendix F, the note on how the work was done and Section 3.2 are updated; see lean/README.md.
- **Correction:** Appendix A stated the resource-preservation step for extraction in the wrong direction: it said that compression to the source corner of mass 1/81 cannot create a finite positive moment from an infinite full moment. The proofs need the converse, which the text now proves: a finite full moment gives a finite moment of the extracted realization. The source corner $f$ commutes with the marginal density $h$, so $\mathrm{Tr}[(hf)^\alpha]=\mathrm{Tr}(h^\alpha f)\le\mathrm{Tr}\,h^\alpha$, and after normalization by the mass 1/81 the corner's $\alpha$-moment is at most $81^\alpha\,\mathrm{Tr}\,h^\alpha$; the block decomposition gives $S(h_0)\le81\,S(h)$ for entropy. This is the direction used in Sections 9.2 and 10.4. No theorem statement changes.
- **Credit correction for $I_{3322}$** (Sections 1.3 and 8): the two citation lists are now identical. Pál and Vértesi, Phys. Rev. A 82, 022116 (2010), are cited as the origin: they computed the value numerically, with finite-dimensional strategies converging to it, and conjectured that no finite dimension attains it. Non-attainment in finite dimension is credited to Pauwels, Coladangelo and Douglas, and attainment by an infinite-dimensional spatial strategy to Coladangelo and Douglas. Krohn-Grimberghe is cited only for his claimed computer-assisted exact identification of the value. The Douglas reference now gives its Zenodo record (doi:10.5281/zenodo.21782008, all versions) before the arXiv number.
- The abstract now states that $\Sigma^0_2$-completeness of spatial perfection, with and without finite entanglement entropy, is verified in Lean. It also states Theorem D: for every computable $0<\alpha<1$, perfect play with $\mathrm{Tr}\,h^\alpha<\infty$ is $\Sigma^0_2$-complete, and these conditions form a strict ladder between finite rank and finite entropy.
- Self-citations point to the latest versions: the entropy-game paper at version 1.1.0 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23932017) and the type-game paper at version 1.1.0 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23931154). Every theorem, lemma, equation and section number cited from them is unchanged in those versions.
