\section*{How this work was done}\label{sec:methods}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How this work was done}
Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 assisted with derivations, checking, programming and writing. The authors selected the questions, directed the work and take responsibility for the paper. The semifinite-but-not-spatial completion, the moment ladder and the nested compiler were derived with models from one developer and checked by a model from the other; these checks were automated.

Exact and numerical checks of the finite identities, counts and examples used in the proofs are in the repository,
\begin{center}\codearchive,\end{center}
and Appendix~\ref{sec:repro} describes their scope. Appendix~\ref{app:leanA} describes the formal verification of Theorem~A, together with the selected formalized lemmas for Theorems~B--D.
