\section{Software checks}\label{sec:repro}
The finite checks are in the directory \texttt{verification/} of the repository below, and the Lean development of Appendix~\ref{app:leanA} is in \texttt{lean/}. The proofs in this paper do not depend on the finite checks. They test finite instances of the identities, constants and constructions; \texttt{verification/DETAILS.md} records every check and count.

\centerline{\codearchive}

\paragraph{Checked exactly.} Exact rational or algebraic arithmetic checks
\begin{itemize}
\item the tree, clock and reader identities on ten finite models (depths $0$--$4$, clock orders $2$ and $3$), where exactly the four top-depth isometry rows fail, as \eqref{eq:fourdefects} predicts;
\item a compiled four-bridge instance with one modular isometry and no transported labels ($748$ rational rows, $27\,991$ questions per party), and the worked tableau of Table~\ref{tab:toy};
\item the scaffold constants $4n+1$, $6n+1$ and $\mathfrak c_n$, the five- and eight-bridge constants \eqref{eq:fiveconstant} and \eqref{eq:eightconstant}, the code entropy \eqref{eq:fiveentropy} and the Catalan identities \eqref{eq:Catnumbers};
\item finite binary trees, reader stand-ins and chain scaffold masses for the type rows, and the Boolean profiles of every reduction in Theorem~\ref{thm:differences};
\item the finite selector models of Section~\ref{sec:iionly}, and the tree spectra and comb arithmetic of Sections~\ref{sec:renyi}--\ref{sec:programmable};
\item five literal instances of the nested compiler of Appendix~\ref{nested:sec:nested} (three predicates covering both decision branches and two comb programs, with $84\,402$ to $103\,474$ rational rows each), every row evaluated in the rational free group ring, and a synthetic presentation with $518\,601$ rows that exercises every translation route;
\item the rotation margin of Appendix~\ref{app:rotationcertificate}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Checked in floating point.} With explicit tolerances: the truncated Fock model with its Weyl pair and tableau; good primitive phases for every prime below $4000$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:goodphase} is the proof for all primes); the trace-class correspondence on the magic square and the flagged magic square, including the negative control for the marginal $D^2$ mentioned after Theorem~\ref{thm:correspondence}; the reader bounds and reductions of Section~\ref{sec:iionly}; the Fibonacci clock models of orders $3$, $5$ and $8$; randomized group-law and nested transport identities.

\paragraph{Not checked.} None of the finite checks proves infinite-dimensional existence or extraction, compactness, the general compiler, the Higman and HNN embedding theorems, the classification of type-III factors, or any hardness reduction. No full reduction game is written out; the questions of the full instance in Section~\ref{sec:reduction} are only counted. The finite group stand-ins do not represent the groups $\mathcal G_e$ of Section~\ref{sec:group}.

\paragraph{How to run.} In \texttt{verification/}, run \texttt{python -B run\_all.py -{}-output-dir DIR} with \texttt{DIR} new or empty; add \texttt{-{}-full-counts} to repeat both question counts. The runner regenerates the nested instances, compares counts and hashes with the shipped records, and fails if any check fails. Its \texttt{README.md} lists dependencies and maps the program labels to the equations of this paper. The paper itself builds with \texttt{python -B build.py} in \texttt{paper/}.

\section{An exact rotation margin}\label{app:rotationcertificate}
For $\vartheta=(\sqrt5-1)/2$ and $\zeta=e^{2\pi i\vartheta}$, use the lower bound of Section~\ref{sec:weylsemifinite}.
Put $\gamma_y=2^{y+1}|\zeta^y-1|$. The global minimum of $\log_2 \gamma_y$ is attained at $y=1$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:margin}
 2.89<\log_2 \gamma_1<2.9,\qquad
 \log_2 \gamma_1=2.898452049757\ldots.
\end{equation}
Here is a certificate, including the infinite tail. For $y\geq3$, the preceding bound gives $\gamma_y\geq2^{y+3}/(\sqrt5\,y+1/2)>8$: at $y=3$ use $\sqrt5<9/4$, and the ratio increases because $2(\sqrt5\,y+1/2)>\sqrt5\,(y+1)+1/2$. For $y=2$ the exact certificate described below gives $\gamma_2>8$. For $y=1$ it gives $1<\gamma_1<8$ and $\gamma_{1,-}^{100}>2^{289}$, $\gamma_{1,+}^{10}<2^{29}$ for rational bounds $\gamma_{1,-}<\gamma_1<\gamma_{1,+}$. To construct them, enclose $\sqrt5$ by integer square roots at scale $10^{80}$, and $\pi$ by $16\arctan(1/5)-4\arctan(1/239)$ with 60 alternating terms and the next-term remainder. Evaluate $\sin(\pi\vartheta)$ at the rational endpoints using 40 alternating terms and the next term; sine is decreasing on this interval in $(\pi/2,\pi)$. Round outward to denominator $10^{40}$ and compare the displayed integer powers. For $y=2$, use $|\sin(2\pi\vartheta)|=\sin(\pi(2\vartheta-1))$; its argument is in $(0,\pi/2)$, where sine increases, and the same series gives $16\sin(\pi(2\vartheta-1))>8$. The exact-fraction routine in \texttt{verification/types/} carries out these steps. It certifies the $y=1,2$ enclosures and the rational base and coefficient inequality of the tail estimate. Together with the induction above these prove the minimum over all positive integers. The weaker positive margin of Section~\ref{sec:weylsemifinite} is all that contractivity requires.
